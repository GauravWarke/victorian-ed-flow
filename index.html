Evidence and calculations

Definitions, sources and limitations

Each rate is the department’s published percentage of the relevant patient group meeting its time standard. Category 1 covers the most urgent group. Handover measures ambulance arrival to completed hospital handover. Length of stay covers the whole ED visit. Percentage-point shortfall = target minus actual. Annual movement = current minus previous result. The trend uses actual results only. Aggregate rates cannot establish the cause of delays or individual care quality.

Derived measures

Annual movement is this year’s result minus last year’s, in percentage points. The share outside a standard is 100% minus the reported result. Target attainment counts measures meeting their own target, divided by the four measures shown. Rounding follows the published inputs.
