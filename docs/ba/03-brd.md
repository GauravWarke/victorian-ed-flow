## Scope and intended use
This annual review helps Victorian emergency care teams choose where to investigate. It shows which standards were met and how results changed over time. Individual triage, hospital rankings, staffing decisions and forecasts require other evidence.

BR-01 (BO-01): distinguish achieved targets and rank shortfalls. Source: service review brief and dashboard.
BR-02 (BO-02): distinguish annual movement from differences in patient groups and make each comparison reproducible. Source: handover, ED stay and evidence pages.
SR-01: A service lead needs a priority and a local follow-up question (BR-01).
SR-02: An analyst needs dated observations and source references (BR-02).

## Constraints and dependencies
Use published annual aggregate data. Keep the offline dashboard independent of remote assets. See A-01 and D-01 in the RAID log.
