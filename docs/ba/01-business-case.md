## Decision
Which part of emergency care should a Victorian service investigate first? Start with time spent in the ED. In 2025–26, 49.8% of visits ended within four hours. That was 25.2 percentage points below the 75% target. Source: annual report, pp. 214–215, linked in the business requirements document.

## Options
Reading the annual reports takes the least upkeep, but each reviewer has to match the measures across years. The dashboard puts those comparisons in one place. A hospital that wants to understand the delays will need its own encounter records.

## Objectives and success measures
BO-01: At release, identify the largest shortfall across all four 2025–26 measures and trace it to a published result.
BO-02: At release, show the direction of change for both three-year series and identify exactly which years support each conclusion.
Baseline for both checks: manual review of annual publications; time taken has not been measured. Test against the acceptance cases in the traceability matrix.

## Value and limits
A service team can use the statewide result to choose its first question. To find where a wait occurred and why, the team needs hospital and shift records. Build cost, time saved and changes in patient flow have yet to be measured.
