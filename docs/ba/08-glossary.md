ED means emergency department. Financial years end in June. A percentage point is the arithmetic difference between two percentages. A register row represents one measure in one financial year.
    
| Measure or display | Calculation and unit | Decision use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Four-hour stay | Published share of ED visits under four hours (0–100%) | Scope the total-stay review |
| Ambulance handover | Published share of ambulance transfers completed within 40 minutes (0–100%) | Review transfer delays |
| Treatment within triage time | Published share treated within their triage time standard (0–100%) | Review access to first treatment |
| Category 1 | Published share of category 1 patients treated immediately (0–100%) | Monitor the most urgent group |
| Gap bars and target markers | Target minus actual, percentage points; zero means target met | Rank shortfalls; compare each actual bar with its target marker on a 0–100% scale |
| Four attainment tiles | Count actual ≥ target; one tile per standard | Count achieved standards |
| Trend lines | Published annual percentages; labelled chart scale 40–80% | Compare direction within each series |
| Stay split | 100 minus the under-four-hour share; bars total 100% | Estimate the share staying longer |
| Coverage matrix | Presence or absence of a register row | Determine which trends are supported |

Source for every display: data/annual-results.csv and its row-level publication links. The register gives rounded percentages. To count patients or measure an individual wait, use the underlying encounter records.
